# Varia Studio — Founder Operating System v2
**Effective:** 14 November 2026 · **Commercial model:** Service-led, product-supported applied AI and geospatial engineering

## Outcome
Build the first repeatable paid B2B engineering workflow. Varia is not yet a product-led SaaS company with proven recurring demand. Near-term work must establish a buyer, real problem, paid scope, delivery evidence and a reference.

## Operating principles
1. One proposition: applied AI and geospatial engineering for agriculture, soil and environmental data.
2. Sell scoped engineering outcomes, not experimental product metrics.
3. Vision and Carbon are evidence assets at different validation stages, not the whole commercial offer.
4. Research is not qualification; only founder-reviewed evidence can move a lead to `qualified`.
5. Natasha drafts; the founder decides whether to send.
6. Every active lead has one next action and date.
7. No customer, revenue, scientific or performance claim without evidence and permission.
8. No new product work unless tied to a paid customer need or critical evidence gap.

## Priority ICP
AgTech, drone-data, GIS and geospatial service firms with existing customers and active agriculture/soil/environment/remote-sensing projects, especially where they need geospatial pipelines, remote-sensing processing, ML integration, APIs, backend systems or analytics dashboards. Secondary: soil/environment consultancies and agricultural software teams with a specific funded workflow.

## Offers
- Technical Diagnostic (`audit`): paid workflow/data/architecture review.
- Engineering Sprint (`pilot`): fixed-scope paid implementation or PoC.
- API / Data Integration (`api_white_label`): paid technical discovery and integration.
- Discovery Pending (`discovery_pending`): no quote until problem, buyer and scope are clear.

Use `Other` in the product field for general studio-service leads. Keep product-specific opportunities under Vision, Carbon or Trait-Bridge.

## Daily operating rhythm
**Start — 15 minutes:** check replies and due actions; review no more than three new Natasha research records; choose the one account that merits attention.

**Commercial block — 60–90 minutes:** research or contact high-fit accounts; prepare one company-specific message at a time; log actual sends and replies; set a next action only when there is a real reason and date.

**Delivery/evidence block:** maintain one demo-ready workflow; improve technical evidence only when it supports a buyer conversation or removes a claim risk; no feature-building without buyer signal.

**Close — 10 minutes:** update stages and activities; record cash received/expenses; leave tomorrow's highest-value action visible.

## Weekly review
Review researched and founder-reviewed accounts, verified contacts, first touches, positive replies, meetings booked/held, scoped opportunities, proposals, signed engagements, cash received, delivery hours, gross margin and loss reasons.

## 30-day first-customer target
Primary outcome: one or two paid B2B engineering engagements with documented scope and payment. This is a target, not a forecast. Optimize for one buyer with a costly workflow problem, access to budget and a feasible delivery scope — not a large prospect list.
